## Dropped (no public-domain source read)

- **Reamker** (text, tradition, transmission). I found no pre-1931 Aymonier or Leclère scan, and EB1911 *Cambodia* does not name it.
- **Kakawin Ramayana** (text, tradition, transmission). EB1911 *Java* lists Kawi works but not a Ramayana. Kern's 1900 edition was not read. The "based on the Bhattikavya" claim is not stored.
- **Paumacariya** (Jain). Jacobi's 1914 edition is not available in a readable copy. Winternitz's work in German was not read.
- **Ramayana reliefs at Prambanan and Angkor Wat.** The sources describe the reliefs only as "legendary episodes".
- **`bangkok-grand-palace` / Ramakien murals.** EB1911 *Bangkok* mentions the palace but not the murals or Wat Phra Kaew.
- **Ayutthaya's fall in 1767.** No end date is stored.
- **Padmasambhava and the Indian teachers in Tibet.** Not in the article that was read.

## Disagreements and caveats

- EB1911 dates Prambanan inconsistently (a tradition of 1266-1296 against Ijzerman's 8th-10th century). No date is stored.
- Macdonell treats the Dasaratha Jataka as knowing the Ramayana. He also notes the opposite view, that it is the older text.
- Some map origins are conveniences, as each record states: Bodh Gaya to Anuradhapura, Gandhara to Khotan, Anuradhapura to Ayutthaya.
- Author death dates still need checking: Maxwell's death (1897) and the EB article authors.
